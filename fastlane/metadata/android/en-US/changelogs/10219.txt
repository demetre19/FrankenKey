Fixed inverted autocorrect learning: undoing an autocorrect no longer teaches the misspelling you rejected, and repeated unusual words go through the Keep/Use best/Replace review instead of silently joining your vocabulary. Numbers keep their dots — decimals and versions like 5.5 and v2.0 no longer lose the period. Learned Words gains Export (taught words, adaptive words, correction rules to a text file) and Delete list (bulk-unlearn from a text file in one pass, correction history included). Next-word context learning is revived with time-decay for stale predictions, and rejecting a correction unwinds its inherited evidence. Suggestions now appear in every non-secret text field; email and URL fields get suggestions without automatic rewriting.
