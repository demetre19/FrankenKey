===========================

Files:

  SHA-256 f58af12ce85b4198362d5a06ccaa6505df3428930feea1a1a661b9df9ca45757

The files are byte-for-byte decompressed copies of the gzip-compressed Cdict
artifacts published by Unexpected Keyboard Dictionaries at commit
c71e034c3fd70024ffa90c9fbfebb686503ff771, format v1:
https://github.com/Julow/Unexpected-Keyboard-dictionaries/tree/c71e034c3fd70024ffa90c9fbfebb686503ff771/v1

That project builds the dictionaries from Helium314/aosp-dictionaries:
https://codeberg.org/Helium314/aosp-dictionaries

The upstream project is licensed under GNU GPL version 3. The complete licence
is included in assets/licenses/aosp-dictionaries-gpl3.txt.

Derived word frequency tables
=============================

Files:
- assets/language_packs/en_AU/word_frequencies.bin
  SHA-256 d8a03df66a7c3f8999dd6be0f39a25e9ed52bcd36e818bbe8497cc78ecd8145b
- assets/language_packs/en_GB/word_frequencies.bin
  SHA-256 15914e5d30adfbd6e3cd196f33e2ef6bc6f864cd463e77a8ebe72740dec69266
- assets/language_packs/en_US/word_frequencies.bin
  SHA-256 bec617549d32bf31782827ea577fb1028c9be73346db4a6f445dce038e7af6f3

Generated by scripts/gen_word_frequencies.py from the same AOSP wordlists the
Cdict files above were built from (Helium314/aosp-dictionaries commit
48c29a96b8e8b66f93d8c89e964c696a2c045ad5, wordlists/main_<locale>.combined;
SHA-256 en_US 73308519ff089c73baa35e08f25087bb62ed4a6bf2f96af3efb402187f7c9edb,
en_GB 4716c9ec06b579f2c9c78ccd114a61865eb117d6b8bf125f20cfd80ce4fe199b,
en_AU 7354e155590b9f635cbb3f2d67e9677b7c2c6f4abb74869e658571a67746902a).
They hold each word's original 0-255 frequency, indexed by Cdict word index.
They are needed because the Cdict builder packs frequency into 4 bits and wraps
its top cluster to 0 (the, and, of, to, in, a and was), a bug fixed in
vendor/cdict/ocaml-cdict/builder/k_medians.ml for future rebuilds. These
tables are licensed under GNU GPL version 3, like their source.

- assets/language_packs/en_GB/word_bigrams.bin

  SHA-256 5a0d42ac2bbe77792b3c679a94a741ad67e272563009fcaab58716c1e1fb8349

Generated by scripts/gen_word_bigrams.py from the bigram lines of the same
pinned wordlist (main_en_US.combined; the en_GB and en_AU wordlists carry no
bigrams, so all three locales use en_US continuations mapped onto their own
Cdict indexes). Head words with source frequency of at least 80 keep up to
three ranked continuations. These tables are licensed under GNU GPL version
3, like their source.
