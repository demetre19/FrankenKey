Bundled English Hunspell sources
================================

The bundled English (Australia), English (United Kingdom), and English
(United States) Hunspell files under assets/language_packs are derived from
LibreOffice dictionaries commit:
da8a7e73fd26a134ad7c6438fa7c310730906b3a
https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/da8a7e73fd26a134ad7c6438fa7c310730906b3a/en

FrankenKey adds missing lowercase entries so every pack recognizes the
technical names gboard, omp, cmux, npm, pnpm, adb, and localhost plus the
chat abbreviations lol, lmao, brb, idk, tbh, and omg. Existing upstream
entries are retained where present. The .aff files remain unmodified.
