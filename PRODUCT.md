# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Android phone users who want a fast, private keyboard and an integrated reading workflow. They use FrankenKey in ordinary apps, terminals, small phone screens, and accessibility-sensitive contexts where the keyboard must preserve application content and vertical space.

## Product Purpose

FrankenKey provides capable everyday and coding-oriented input without ads or tracking, plus an optional Reader for listening to text and saved documents. Success means common actions are immediately understandable, private data stays local, and keyboard or Reader controls never obscure more of the host app than their current task requires.

## Brand Personality

Private, capable, compact. The voice is direct and approachable rather than technical or promotional. User-facing names describe people and outcomes, not implementation identifiers.

## Anti-references

- Technical engine or locale identifiers exposed as primary labels.
- Tall single-column action stacks that waste limited phone height.
- Persistent controls for inactive features.
- Redundant buttons that compete with content or headings.
- Cramped navigation where icons and titles visually touch.
- Decorative complexity that weakens readability or touch confidence.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the host app: reveal only controls needed for the current state.
- Make compact layouts predictable: equal-width grids, consistent spacing, and stable touch targets.
- Keep text visibly inset: bordered inputs and learned-word rows reserve at least 12dp between text and their inner horizontal edges.
- Prefer human language: friendly names and clear outcomes over internal identifiers.
- Design small-screen first: respect system and IME insets and keep content scrollable above overlays.
- Keep privacy visible through behavior, not extra chrome.
- Treat vocabulary as intentional personal data: learn words only through explicit teaching or a deliberate correction choice after editing that word, never from ordinary typing or automatic autocorrection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Meet WCAG AA-equivalent contrast for text and controls.
- Keep interactive targets at least 48dp.
- Provide descriptive accessibility labels for icon-only actions.
- Support narrow phones, larger text, and IME/window insets without clipped content.
- Preserve clear focus order and state-dependent control visibility.
